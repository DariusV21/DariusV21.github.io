    Teeth use a standard involute profile: addendum 1×module, dedendum 1.25×module, with a root fillet on external gears. Meshing gears always share module and pressure angle, so changing either on one gear updates its whole meshing group. Gears on the same axle can use a different module.

    DXF files are in millimetres (R12 format) and open in AutoCAD, Fusion, FreeCAD, LibreCAD and laser/CNC software. To get a DWG, open the DXF in AutoCAD or DWG TrueView and save as DWG. For printed gears set a little backlash (0.05–0.15 mm) so they don't bind.
