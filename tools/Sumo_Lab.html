Sumo Lab

Build, program and fight mini sumo robots

Your robot

Every robot shares the same 10 × 10 cm, 500 g wedge body. You pick one upgrade, and every upgrade costs the same: +2 in one area and −1 in another, because the weight limit never moves. Changing it loads a matching starter program.

Upgrade

Rules run top to bottom. At every moment the highest rule whose trigger is true takes over and can interrupt any rule below it. A rule never interrupts itself or a rule above it. Time 0 s means hold that speed until another rule takes over.

Triggers

Actions

Drag blocks into the program, or tap a block to add it to the selected rule. Drag by ⠿ to reorder. Drop on the palette to delete.
