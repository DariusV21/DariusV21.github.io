    What converts, and how

    Resistors, capacitors (plain and polarized), inductors, diodes, Zeners, LEDs, batteries and DC/sine sources, switches and push buttons, NPN/PNP transistors, N/P MOSFETs and op-amps become Falstad parts. Fuses, ferrite beads and net ties become plain wires.

    Wires keep their KiCad routing. Where a wire end, pin or label touches the middle of another wire, the wire is split, because Falstad only connects at end points. Net labels become Falstad labeled nodes, GND symbols become grounds, and power symbols like +5V or +3V3 become a voltage rail. VCC and VDD are assumed to be 5 V.

    Transistors and op-amps are placed on their KiCad pins with short wires to Falstad’s fixed pin layout, so check their orientation after import. ICs, connectors and anything else are skipped and listed below.

    Parts marked DNP or “Exclude from simulation” are skipped. Copied selections need KiCad 6 or newer.
