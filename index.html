01 / About

Hi, I'm Darius

My main focus is electronics, 3D printing and CAD design – always with manufacturing in mind, so a part or board that looks good on screen can actually be made, assembled and repeated.

I'm also a beginner robotics enthusiast. I design my own robots end to end: the mechanical CAD, the electronics and the PCBs. Some of that work is shared in collaboration with lithuanianbots.com.

And from time to time I step away from the soldering iron for some woodworking – boxes, shelves and other small fixtures made out of wood.

  • Electronics & PCB

    Schematics, custom PCB design and embedded firmware.

  • 3D printing

    FDM and SLA parts, enclosures and functional prints.

  • CAD for manufacturing

    Parametric models designed to be printed, cut or assembled.

  • Robotics

    Own robot designs – CAD, electronics and PCBs.

  • Woodworking

    Boxes, shelves and small fixtures, now and then.
